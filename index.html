          How this works

          Estimates only, not financial advice. The planner assumes your APRs stay the same and you stop using the cards. Each card's minimum is the larger of $25 and 1% of the balance plus that month's interest. Each plan is one fixed monthly payment: every card gets its minimum, the rest goes to the priority card, and when a card is paid off its payment moves to the next one.

          Plan ratings compare the payment with the money left after your bills and safety cushion. "Very likely" uses up to 60% of it, "Likely" fits within it, "Tight" dips into your cushion, and "Too much" needs more than you have left. Loan estimates use a standard fixed payment, with the upfront fee taken out of the loan amount. Your data stays in this browser.
