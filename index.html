        Estimates only. The planner assumes your APR stays the same, you make no new purchases, and each card's minimum is the greater of $25 (or the minimum you enter) and 1% of the balance plus that month's interest. Your card agreement may calculate it differently. Likelihood ratings compare each payment with the money left after your essentials and cushion: "Very likely" uses up to 60% of what's left after your cushion, "Likely" fits within it, "A stretch" dips into the cushion, and "Not affordable" needs more than you have left. Loan estimates use a standard fixed-rate payment, with the origination fee taken out of the loan amount. This is not financial advice.
